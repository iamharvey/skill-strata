# 回复范式（示意）

| decision | 写法 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| RESHIP_PARTS | 确认配件编号，说明物流单号另行发送 | We'll send part C4 right away. You'll receive a tracking number by email. |
| OFFER | 并列列出选项，请客户选择 | We can send a replacement table top, or offer a 15% partial refund. Which works best for you? |
| HANDOFF | 说明已转专人，不解释内部原因 | Thanks for the details. A specialist will follow up with you shortly. |
| HANDOFF (safety) | 只致歉并说明专人联系，不谈责任与赔偿 | We're very sorry. A specialist will contact you as soon as possible. |
